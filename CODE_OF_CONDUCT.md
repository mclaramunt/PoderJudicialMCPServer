# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and help keep
this a welcoming place for people of every background and experience level.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer
privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/mclaramunt). Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions,
or block people, who break this code.
